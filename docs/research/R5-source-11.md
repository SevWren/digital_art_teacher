 Academics

 4 Year, Full-time Program
 Undergraduate BFA
 A Bachelor of Fine Arts program that covers multiple aspects of a 3D generalist skillset, providing an in-depth understanding of the 3D production pipeline, visual arts, and general education studies.
 Learn More | Apply Now

 2-Year, Full-time Program
 Certificate in Digital Production for Entertainment
 An intensive 2-year certificate program, built on a 3D generalist foundation, offering emphasized studies in Game Art, Modeling and Texturing, Visual Effects Animation, or Character and Creature Animation.
 Learn More | Apply Now

 10-week, On-Campus & Online
 Individual Courses
 Standalone avocational courses covering a wide variety of subjects within film, game, and visual effects production. Experience the same innovative training and instruction as our program courses both on-campus or online.
 Learn More | Browse Courses

 Portfolio Development, Avocational
 Foundation in Art & Design
 Up to one year of fundamental art education for individuals 18 and over looking to become better artists and build a well-rounded portfolio.

 Gnomon's Admissions Advisors are committed to helping you find the most appropriate educational path to meet your goals.
