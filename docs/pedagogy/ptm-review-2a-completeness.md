**Term:** colour profile / ICC profile
**Found in:** `ART THEORY & DIGITAL PRACTICE REFERENCE WIP.md` (§6.3 "convert to the printer's requested profile"; "Soft-proofing (previewing the output profile)"; examples: "U.S. Sheetfed Coated and Web Coated / SWOP variants")
**Why it needs an entry:** A colour profile (ICC profile) is a specific technical file that tells software how to translate your colours for a particular screen or printer; a hobbyist preparing files for print will encounter it and will not understand why they are being asked to "attach a profile" or "convert to profile."
**Suggested plain name:** Colour profile — a small file that tells your software exactly how a specific screen or printer understands colour, so colours match what you see when the file is shared or printed

1. **"aerial perspective"** appears in `fundamentals/perspective.md` and `ART THEORY & DIGITAL PRACTICE REFERENCE WIP.md` as a synonym for atmospheric perspective. The PTM has a separate "Aerial perspective" entry noting it is a synonym — good — but the Atmospheric perspective entries (there are two, in both the Light and Value section and the Perspective section) should cross-reference it explicitly.

2. **"skeletal landmarks"** is the corpus term in `fundamentals/anatomy-and-gesture.md`; the PTM uses "Landmark bones." Add "skeletal landmarks" as an alias in the Landmark bones entry.

3. **"action line"** is used in `ART THEORY & DIGITAL PRACTICE REFERENCE WIP.md` (§3.8) as a synonym for "line of action." The PTM entry for Line of action should list "action line" as an alias.

4. **"local colour blocking"** appears in `ART THEORY & DIGITAL PRACTICE REFERENCE WIP.md` (§5.7 "Local color blocking and palette lock") as a pipeline stage name; the PTM "flat colour stage" covers the same thing. Add "local colour blocking" as an alias in the flat colour stage entry.

5. **"contact darkening"** is used in `ART THEORY & DIGITAL PRACTICE REFERENCE WIP.md` (§5.6 "contact darkening") as a synonym for occlusion/contact shadow. Add "contact darkening" as an alias in the Occlusion shadow / Contact shadow entry.

6. **"form modelling"** appears in `fundamentals/light-and-value.md` ("the main form modelling") and `ART THEORY & DIGITAL PRACTICE REFERENCE WIP.md` as the process of using value to describe three-dimensional form; it is used as a technical term but is effectively covered by the rendering/shading stage and form shadow entries. Add "form modelling" as an alias cross-reference in the rendering/shading stage entry.

7. **"mark-making"** appears in `ART THEORY & DIGITAL PRACTICE REFERENCE WIP.md` (§3.1, §5.9 "Vary mark length, opacity, and direction with the turning of the form") as a term for deliberate brush-stroke choices. It is partially covered by the detail pass and soft/hard brush entries but not as a named concept. Consider adding "mark-making" as an alias or note in the detail pass entry.

8. **"visual texture"** appears in `ART THEORY & DIGITAL PRACTICE REFERENCE WIP.md` (§3.1 "Texture — Visual texture is created by mark-making patterns") and `fundamentals/composition.md` as distinct from physical texture. It is not named or defined separately in the PTM; the Pattern entry comes closest. Consider adding a brief note distinguishing physical/actual texture from visual texture implied by mark-making.

9. **"figure-ground relationship"** (as listed under Gestalt grouping in the corpus) — the corpus at §4.1 and `fundamentals/composition.md` lists figure-ground as one of the Gestalt grouping principles. The PTM Gestalt grouping entry does not list it alongside proximity, similarity, continuity, and closure. The standalone Figure–ground PTM entry exists, but the Gestalt grouping entry should cross-reference it to make the set complete for a learner reading through the Gestalt entry.
