## Pipeline Structure

Informed by common professional digital-illustration and concept-art pipelines (brief → thumbnail → construction → value → colour → render → polish), as described across multiple studio and educational accounts of the illustration and concept-art process.

## Fundamentals

- Composition, value hierarchy, and notan: standard design principles taught in illustration and concept-art curricula.
- Light and form (key/fill/rim, core shadow, cast shadow, reflected light): classical observational painting practice, consistent across academic and entertainment-art training.
- Perspective (linear and atmospheric): standard technical drawing and observational practice.
- Colour (hue/value/saturation, temperature, simultaneous contrast): classical colour theory as applied in both traditional and digital painting education.
- Construction from primary forms and planar analysis: methods common to figure drawing, industrial design, and concept-art training.
- Gesture and structural anatomy for artists: approaches standard in figure-drawing education focused on action and mass rather than medical detail.
