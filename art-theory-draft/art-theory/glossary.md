# Glossary

**Status:** Work in progress. Terms are added as the knowledge base grows.

**Atmospheric perspective** — Reduction of contrast, saturation, and detail with distance, often accompanied by a shift toward the colour of the intervening atmosphere.

**Cast shadow** — Shadow projected by a form onto another surface.

**Core shadow** — The darkest region of a form’s own shadow, where the surface turns away from the light and receives minimal reflected light.

**Gesture** — The overall action, weight, and rhythm of a pose, expressed economically before detailed construction.

**Hierarchy** — The ordered ranking of visual importance within an image, usually established by contrast, isolation, or scale.

**Horizon line** — The eye-level line in linear perspective; vanishing points for horizontal edges lie on it.

**Key light** — The primary directional light that defines the main modelling of forms.

**Notan** — The reduction of an image to a small number of value families (commonly two or three) to test compositional structure.

**Occlusion / Contact shadow** — The darkest value where two surfaces meet and block ambient light.

**Primary forms** — Box, cylinder, sphere (and simple variants) used as the starting construction of more complex objects.

**Value family** — A group of related lightness values kept distinct from other groups (e.g., light family vs shadow family).

**Vanishing point** — The point toward which a set of parallel edges appears to converge in linear perspective.
